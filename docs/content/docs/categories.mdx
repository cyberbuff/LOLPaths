Categories are intentionally broad and stable. Use `subcategory` for products,
providers, and technologies.

Initial categories include:

- `cloud`
- `ssh`
- `source-control`
- `containers`
- `kubernetes`
- `cicd`
- `package-manager`
- `database`
- `secret-manager`
- `infrastructure`
- `certificates`
- `shell`
- `browser`
- `crypto-wallet`
- `development`

## Sensitivity

- `critical`: directly reusable authentication material
- `high`: highly likely to expose credentials or privileged operational data
- `medium`: sensitive configuration or useful operational detail
- `low`: contextual information useful for discovery
- `informational`: limited sensitivity on its own

Choose the lower value when evidence is weak.
